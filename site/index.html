01

课堂难点

Where notes fall short

有录音
不等于有证据

A recording is only the start
of checking what was said

老师讲到重点和考点时，信息往往连续出现，笔记很难同时跟上。录音能留下现场，但噪声、距离和多人说话也可能让转写漏字或偏离原话。

When a teacher reaches a key point or exam cue, information can arrive faster than notes can keep up. A recording keeps the moment, but noise, distance, and overlapping voices can still pull a transcript away from the original words.

课溯先把原始录音留下，再在本机完成转写。整理重点时，用课程材料和专业词库校对；想确认时，点回时间点听原音。

VeriLecture keeps the original recording and transcribes it on the device. Course material and subject terms help check the points; when something is unclear, jump back to the timestamp and listen.

Traceability

A review point is not the end
check it against the audio

课溯把原始内容、修订版本和复习重点分开保存。每条重点都带有转写片段和时间点；有疑问时，回到原音核对。

The source, edited versions, and review points stay separate. Each point keeps its transcript segment and timestamp, so you can check the audio when something is unclear.

A

原始内容单独保留

Keep the source separate

原始音频和原始转写不被覆盖。修订和编辑会保存为新版本。

The original audio and transcript are not overwritten. Revisions and edits are saved as new versions.

B

Teacher statements stay separate from inference

考点、不考内容、重复强调和系统推测分别显示。

Exam points, exclusions, repeated emphasis, and system inference are shown separately.
