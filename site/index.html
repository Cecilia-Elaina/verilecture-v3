01

为什么做课溯

Why VeriLecture

AI 可以更快
但证据不能丢

AI can move faster
Evidence must stay

一段课堂录音，往往要经过转写、纠错、整理，最后才变成可用的笔记。普通工具只给你结果，课溯把中间的来路也一起保留下来。

A lecture becomes useful only after transcription, correction, and organization. Most tools keep only the result. VeriLecture keeps the path that produced it.

不是一份“神奇摘要”，而是一条可以核对的学习证据链。 Not a magic summary — a learning trail you can actually verify.
A

原始内容是根

The source is the root

原始音频和原始转写不被覆盖。修正、校准与编辑都会生成带来路的新版本。

Original audio and raw transcripts are never overwritten. Corrections and calibration create new versions with provenance.

B

老师说的 单独放

Teacher statements stay distinct

明确考点、不考内容、重复强调与 AI 推测分开呈现，避免把“可能”写成“老师说”。

Explicit exam points, exclusions, repeated emphasis, and AI inference remain separate — so “possible” never becomes “the teacher said.”

C

授权不是默认勾选

Consent is never assumed

音频、转写或教材片段只有在对应授权被明确记录后，才会进入可选的云端步骤。

Audio, transcripts, or source excerpts enter optional cloud steps only after the matching consent is explicitly recorded.

每一步只做一件事，每一步都保留与上一步的关系。

Each step does one job and keeps its relationship to the step before it.

01

本地优先 是默认路线

Local-first is the default

本地转写不会上传音频；课程文件先在本机解析。

Local transcription does not upload audio, and course files are parsed locally first.

02

授权按内容分别记录

Consent is recorded by content type

音频、转写、教材片段各有自己的授权边界，不会打包成模糊的“同意”。

Audio, transcripts, and excerpts have separate consent boundaries instead of one vague approval.

03

失败也不改写原始内容

Failure never rewrites the source

可选分析失败时，原始音频与转写仍保持原样。

If optional analysis fails, the original audio and transcript remain untouched.
